About

Still early, already hooked

Irvan Adi Nugroho PHOTO PLACEHOLDER

I'm a first-year student at IPB University with a growing obsession with how things get broken into — and how they're kept safe. What started as curiosity about a locked-down school Wi-Fi turned into late nights with Wireshark, home-lab misconfigurations, and beginner CTF challenges.

I don't have a decade of experience, but I have consistency: I read write-ups before bed, I rebuild my lab when I break it, and I ask the annoying "but why does that work" question until I actually understand it.

Right now I'm focused on networking fundamentals, Linux, and the mindset that security is a habit, not a checklist.
